Grazing Table
  • Artisan cheeses, crackers, charcuterie, fruit, nuts, dips, olives, honey
  • Varying heights (boards, stands, crates) for visual interest
  • Keep in shade — cheese melts fast in July
  • Assign 1 person to set up and refresh during the party
Chafing Dish & Ice Logistics

Chafing Dishes

  • 6-8 full-size units (rent or disposable aluminum)
  • Pre-heat 30 min before service
  • Hot food above 140F, cold below 40F — bring a food thermometer
  • Sterno fuel: 2-3 boxes + spare

Ice Plan

  • Assign 1 person as ice + cooler lead — manages refills all night
  • Separate coolers for drinks vs. food safety
  • 150-200 lbs, bought morning-of

Food Replenishment

  • Assign 1-2 food leads — monitor and replenish throughout night
  • Brief them: where food is stored, what order to bring things out
  • Key moments: snacks to dinner transition, dessert, late-night refresh
Coffee & Dessert

Coffee

  • Options: large coffee urn (30-60 cup), cold brew concentrate + pitchers, or skip
  • If yes: regular + decaf, cream/sugar station, assign someone to manage

Dessert

  • Exploring: local bakery items and/or Costco sheet cake(s)
  • Decision TBD
Food Cleanup Plan

Decide in advance what happens to leftover food at end of night.

  • Options: pack into containers for family to take home, compost scraps, trash disposables
  • Clear food service area before late-night fire/hangout phase
  • Assign 1-2 people to handle food breakdown (separate from general cleanup lead)
  • Food safety: anything out 2+ hours in summer heat should be tossed, not saved
