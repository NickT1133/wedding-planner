Property Layout

Map the Property

  • Ceremony area, tent/reception area, parking, porta potties (downwind), trash station, fire pit, yard games zone, photo booth
  • Get aerial view and overlay tent placements + table layout

Ceremony Area

  • Decide exact spot — consider sun angle at 4 PM in July, wind, noise
  • Mark with signage or florals on the day

Table Layout

  • 90-100 guests
  • Long family tables vs. rounds, head table, kids table
  • Food/grazing table, bar table, gift/card table, dessert table
  • Clear pathways to food and restrooms
  • Map food placement per table — serving flow and replenishment access

Dance Floor & Rugs

  • Level ground, away from water
  • Outdoor rugs to define the space
  • Speaker placement nearby
Decor & Lighting

Decor Plan

  • Inventory: what you have, borrowing, and need to buy
  • Centerpieces, ceremony backdrop/arch, signage, tablecloths/runners, candles/lanterns, florals

Lighting

  • Tent lights rented (done)
  • String lights on trees, candles/votives on tables, pathway lighting, fire pit

Favors

  • Ideas: local honey, seed packets, small candles, jam
  • Take-one display near exit or on tables
Entertainment

Playlists (4 needed)

  • Ceremony — processional, recessional, prelude
  • Cocktail/snack hour — upbeat, not too loud
  • Dinner — mellow, conversational
  • Evening/dancing — higher energy

Download playlists to old phone, connect via Bluetooth to speaker. Consider adding a microphone for announcements/toasts. Assign someone to manage music day-of.

Yard Games

  • Cornhole, bocce, horseshoes, giant Jenga, KanJam, lawn bowling

Photo Booth & Slideshow

  • Old iPhone on a mount, shaded spot, fun background
  • Booth app for auto-capture, QR code for shared album
  • Monitor under tent with live slideshow — guests scan QR to upload photos in real time (no app needed)

Guest Photo Album & Live Slideshow

  • Evaluating services: GuestCam ($49-55), Kululu (free-$99), PixelParty ($50-100)
  • QR code upload from guest phones, no app or account required
  • Photos appear on TV/monitor in real-time as a live slideshow
  • All photos downloadable after the event
  • Setup: TV near tents on generator, print QR codes on table cards or sign
  • Test full chain day before: phone upload to slideshow on screen

Flowers

  • Sunflowers with greenery and fillers
  • Possibly delegate arrangements to Kelly
  • Decide: DIY bouquets + table arrangements, or just table?
Gear & Equipment

Quiet Generator

  • Inverter/quiet type required — Honda EU2200i, EU3000is, or Yamaha EF2200iS
  • 1,500-2,500W minimum. Rent locally or buy. 2x 50ft outdoor extension cords.

Speakers

  • Weather-resistant, projecting to ~100 people outdoors
  • QSC K12.2, Bose L1 Compact, or local rental
  • Test full chain (generator + speakers + playlist) 1 week before

Porta Potties

  • 2-3 units, at least 1 ADA-accessible
  • Delivery day before, pickup day after. Confirm handwashing station.

Refrigeration

  • 2x 150qt coolers (arriving Mar 30) — for drinks on the day
  • Used chest freezer (marketplace) — ice stockpile, goes by tents on generator
  • Used fridge (marketplace) — cold food items, goes in garage nearby
  • Stockpile ice in freezer + fridge in the days leading up

Beverage Plan

  • Canned light beers: Coors Light, Bud Light, 1 local craft light
  • Prosecco for toasting
  • Sparkling water / sodas for non-drinkers
  • Quantities TBD — estimate ~2-3 beers per person over 4-5 hrs
