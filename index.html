Gear & Equipment

Quiet Generator

  • Inverter/quiet generator required — standard ones are too loud
  • Options: Honda EU2200i, Honda EU3000is, Yamaha EF2200iS
  • Power needs: 1,500-2,500W minimum
  • Rent locally or buy (good resale). Get 2x 50ft outdoor extension cords.

Speakers

  • Weather-resistant, projecting to ~100 people outdoors
  • Options: QSC K12.2, Bose L1 Compact, or local rental
  • Test full chain (generator + speakers + playlist) at least 1 week before

Porta Potties

  • 2-3 units for 90-100 guests over 5-6 hours
  • At least 1 ADA-accessible unit
  • Delivery day before, pickup day after
  • Place downwind, out of sightlines. Confirm handwashing station.

Refrigeration

  • What needs cold: beer, wine, salads, cut fruit, charcuterie
  • Large insulated coolers + dedicated food safety coolers

Storm Plan

No indoor backup. Tents are the plan: one 20x40, one 10x20, two 10x10 = 1,200 sq ft total. Fits 90-100 seated.

Risk Levels

  • Light rain: Tents handle it. Keep going.
  • Heavy rain: Move fully under tents, close sides.
  • Thunder/lightning: Shelter under tents, pause until it passes.
  • Major storm: Monitor conditions. Consider postponing if severe.

Prep

  • Confirm tent sides are in the rental
  • Weather monitoring from 10 days out; go/no-go at 48 hours
  • Assign weather watcher day-of (NOAA every 30 min)
  • Secure lightweight decor (weights, clips, stakes)
