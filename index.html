Chafing Dish & Ice Logistics

Chafing Dishes

  • 6-8 full-size units (rent or disposable aluminum)
  • Pre-heat 30 min before service
  • Hot food above 140F, cold below 40F — bring a food thermometer
  • Sterno fuel: 2-3 boxes + spare

Ice Plan

  • Assign 1 person as ice + cooler lead — manages refills all night
  • Separate coolers for drinks vs. food safety
  • 150-200 lbs, bought morning-of

Food Replenishment

  • Assign 1-2 food leads — monitor and replenish throughout night
  • Brief them: where food is stored, what order to bring things out
  • Key moments: snacks to dinner transition, dessert, late-night refresh

Gear & Equipment

Quiet Generator

  • Inverter/quiet type required — Honda EU2200i, EU3000is, or Yamaha EF2200iS
  • 1,500-2,500W minimum. Rent locally or buy. 2x 50ft outdoor extension cords.

Speakers

  • Weather-resistant, projecting to ~100 people outdoors
  • QSC K12.2, Bose L1 Compact, or local rental
  • Test full chain (generator + speakers + playlist) 1 week before

Porta Potties

  • 2-3 units, at least 1 ADA-accessible
  • Delivery day before, pickup day after. Confirm handwashing station.

Refrigeration

Storm Plan

No indoor backup. Tents are the plan: one 20x40, one 10x20, two 10x10 = 1,200 sq ft total. Fits 90-100 seated.

Risk Levels

  • Light rain: Tents handle it. Keep going.
  • Heavy rain: Move under tents, close sides.
  • Thunder/lightning: Shelter, pause until it passes.
  • Major storm: Monitor conditions. Consider postponing if severe.

Prep

  • Confirm tent sides are in the rental
  • Weather monitoring from 10 days out; go/no-go at 48 hours
  • Assign weather watcher day-of
  • Secure lightweight decor (weights, clips, stakes)
Emergency Kit

Safety pins, Tide To Go, pain relievers, band-aids, phone chargers + power bank, needle & thread, fashion tape, baby wipes, antacids, sunscreen, bug spray, bobby pins, hair ties, Sharpie
